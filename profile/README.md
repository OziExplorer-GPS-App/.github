**So, what is OziExplorer, exactly?**
OziExplorer is a raster map navigation utility for Windows that turns a scanned or digital map into a live GPS reference you can navigate by. Rather than depending on a generic online map, it lets you work from the exact chart, topo sheet, or property map you already trust, with your current GPS position tracked directly on top of it.

### Is OziExplorer right for you?
- `[Calibration]` Line up any scanned map image or digital chart to real-world coordinates in a few steps.
- `[Live Tracking]` Watch your GPS position, heading, and speed update on the map as you move.
- `[Route Planning]` Build routes and waypoints ahead of a trip, then follow them in the field.
